# Code of Conduct — Contributor Covenant 2.1

This community adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
including its pledge, standards, scope, enforcement responsibilities and guidelines.
The linked standard is the authoritative full policy; this introduction does not
replace it or add legal restrictions.

We welcome people of all backgrounds. Be respectful, empathetic and constructive;
accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, discriminatory
or sexualized conduct, personal attacks and disclosure of others' private information
without permission are unacceptable.

The policy applies in community spaces and when representing the project.
Maintainers may moderate participation using the standard's proportionate correction,
warning and temporary/permanent-ban guidelines.

Privately report conduct incidents to the project maintainer at
[abubakar.meraj11@gmail.com](mailto:abubakar.meraj11@gmail.com), subject
“Video Lesson conduct report”. Reports should be handled fairly and with respect
for privacy. Do not post private incident details publicly.
